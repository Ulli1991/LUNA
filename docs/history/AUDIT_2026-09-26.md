# LUNA audit, 2026-09-26

Branch `sink-stencil` at 173f12e (the tree about to be fast-forwarded onto
`main`), ten days and ~200 commits after the first audit
(`docs/history/AUDIT_2026-09-16.md`). Six read-only reviews in parallel: hydro and the
Pallas kernels, radiation, gravity/sinks/feedback, the Torch coupling
(`src/amuse_luna`), setup/runners/I/O/config, and the status of every item of
the first audit. Nothing was edited or run by the reviewers.

**Verification.** Every finding below carries one of two marks:

- **verified** -- I read the code myself and the failure follows from it;
- **reported** -- a reviewer's reading, not yet independently checked. The
  first audit had one reviewer claim that turned out wrong (its section 12),
  so a *reported* item is a lead, not a fact, until checked.

## 1. Verdict

The solvers the production runs use (split M1, the Pallas kernels, the KDK
gravity, the palindromic step) held up: the reviewers compared every fused
kernel against its XLA path term by term and found them consistent, and 13 of
the 14 ranked bugs of the first audit are fixed. What did not hold up is the
code around the solvers, in three places:

1. **Checkpointing** has no notion of a complete set, and in multi-process runs
   each rank decides on its own when to write one -- a hang that leaves half a
   set behind. The coupled study runs have hit exactly this.
2. **Sinks** were built for one sink at a time: overlapping accretion creates
   mass, periodic boxes are not handled anywhere, standalone checkpoints do not
   carry them, and in coupled runs no one computes star<->sink gravity.
3. **Silent degradations** where a guard is missing: non-cubic cells for
   radiation and sharded gravity, the HLLC solver selecting a one-sided flux when
   a face state goes non-physical, the always-periodic sharded gradient.

## 2. What touches the running feedback/resolution study

These act on the six cloud runs (coupled, periodic 8 pc box, sinks, M1, Torch):

| # | finding | mark | effect on the study |
|---|---|---|---|
| S1 | stars and sinks never feel each other's gravity (coupling) | verified | a sink's unspent reservoir (tens of Msun) is invisible to its stars and to PeTar, and sinks are not pulled by the cluster |
| S2 | the sound speed handed to Torch for newborn stars is sqrt(gamma P/rho), Torch expects sqrt(P/rho) | verified | newborn stars get 1.29x the intended velocity dispersion (gamma = 5/3) |
| S3 | each rank decides on its own when a wall-clock checkpoint is due (coupled worker `IO_out`, and standalone `Outputs.checkpoint`) | verified | a rank-straddled deadline hangs the job inside the write; fits nofb_1024 (half-written set at 16:20, died 16:28) and fb_512 (died 16:35) |
| S4 | overlapping sink accretion windows create (rho - rho_sink)^2/rho dV per shared cell | verified | sinks are not merged and the separation is only checked at creation; how often windows overlap in the runs is unmeasured |
| S5 | sinks have no periodic handling (no wrap, no minimum image in the sink field, the sink-sink sum, the creation distance) | reported | the clouds sit mid-box; matters only for sinks near a face |
| S6 | stars crossing (or born within sink_rad of) a face of the periodic box are deleted by Torch before LUNA's wrap | reported | mass leaves the periodic box with them |
| S7 | the ionising source field is not cleared when the last star is removed | reported | the last photon field keeps shining; rare in the study |
| S8 | kinetic SN injection can make the internal energy negative in cold outflowing gas | reported | relevant once supernovae start (not yet in any run) |

fb_1024's blow-up (dt = -inf at ~1.0 Myr, gas finite, floors active) is **not
explained yet**. Two reviewer mechanisms were checked: the floor/cons_to_prim
rounding (hydro H2) is real but needs |v| ~ 1e7 km/s at the cloud's 10 K floor;
the HLLC one-sided-flux fallback (hydro H1) can manufacture the non-physical
state but does not by itself make the post-floor state non-physical. A probe
measuring max |v|, min rho and P at the failure is running.

## 4. The first audit, ten days on

Of its 14 ranked bugs, **13 are fixed** (with commits) and the 14th (#3, the
1-D/2-D seam) is fixed for BOUNDARY 0/1 and only reachable for 2/4. Of ~90
secondary items: the big structural ones are done -- one time loop
(4848abe), one PM implementation, frozen Config + CLI, the `luna/` package,
checkpoint format v2, CI running the fast tier with lint. Still open:

- non-cubic cells guarded only on the local gravity path (regressed when the
  old files were deleted) -- the same gap as C9;
- the well-balanced integrator's plain jit around a shard_map halo;
- float32 casts in sources (eos_projection, ou_forcing, external_gravity, cc85, turbulence);
- six copies of the half-step predictor; unused HLLC wrappers;
- endgame 2b (one seam construction, 2-ghost reflective fills);
- the sharded implicit adjoint has no 2-device gradient gate;
- docs/CONFIG.md stale (235 fields documented, ~282 exist); README overclaims
  (machine-precision conservation, AMR +0.55%); campaign docs and 83 scaling
  logs still tracked; no tag, no DOI; `config.py.pre-luna` newly tracked;
- U1 (a grouped config with a marked core set) open.

## 5. Proposed order

1. **C2 + C3** (checkpoint decision broadcast from rank 0; write-to-temp,
   rename, complete marker; restart falls back to the newest complete set).
   Small, and the study runs need it now.
2. **C1 + M1** (star<->sink gravity in the coupled bridge; cs = sqrt(P/rho) to
   Torch) -- they change the study's physics, so they need a decision on whether
   the running study continues as is or restarts.
3. **C4 + C7/M3** (sink accretion ordering or a merge; periodic sinks and stars).
4. **C8, C9, M2, C6** (guards: face-state positivity fallback in HLLC; cubic-cell
   checks; the sharded gradient's boundary; the isolated-BC halo).
5. The rest by area, each with its test, teeth-checked.
